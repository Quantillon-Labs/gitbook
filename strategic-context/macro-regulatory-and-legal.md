# Macro, Regulatory & Legal Context

QEURO's first deployment targets euro exposure while using USD-denominated collateral and DeFi infrastructure. This economic design does not establish its legal classification, regulatory authorization or an exemption.

## MiCA context

MiCA generally applies from **30 December 2024**; its asset-referenced-token and e-money-token provisions apply from **30 June 2024**. The applicable obligations depend on the asset and activity, not simply the label "DeFi". See the [EUR-Lex summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/european-crypto-assets-regulation-mica.html).

Recital 22 discusses crypto-asset services provided in a fully decentralized manner without an intermediary. It is not a blanket exemption for every token or a determination that Quantillon is outside MiCA. Token classification, offers and operated services require their own legal analysis. See [Regulation (EU) 2023/1114](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng).

Eligible pre-existing crypto-asset service providers could use transitional arrangements, subject to national choices, until no later than **1 July 2026**, or an earlier authorization/refusal. That date has passed; a historical transition is not evidence of present authorization. See [ESMA, Article 143](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-143-transitional-measures).
