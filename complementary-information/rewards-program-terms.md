# Rewards Program Terms

_Date of the latest revision: 4th of September 2026_\
_Version 1_

These terms govern participation in Quantillon Rewards (the "Program"), a loyalty program operated by Quantillon Labs, a French simplified joint stock company (SAS) with registered office at 2 Avenue de Lognac, 33700 Merignac, France, registered with the Commercial and Companies Register of Bordeaux under number 988 682 613 ("Quantillon Labs", "we"). They complement the [Terms of Service](terms-of-services.md), the [Legal Notice](legal-notice.md) and the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

### 1. Participation

Participation is optional. A user joins the Program by accepting these terms while signed in to the application with a wallet. The version of the terms accepted and the date of acceptance are recorded. One wallet is one participant.

Points can be computed for any wallet that holds QEURO in the protocol, whether or not it has joined. Nothing is displayed, sent or claimable for a wallet that has not joined.

### 2. Points

The Program records points ("QP") that measure, above all, the amount of QEURO held in the protocol over time, with staked QEURO weighted more than QEURO held in a wallet, and, to a bounded extent, the completion of certain actions such as weekly quests, referrals and social tasks.

QP have no monetary value. They cannot be transferred, sold, exchanged or assigned. They do not constitute a claim against Quantillon Labs or the Quantillon protocol and confer no right to any token, payment, allocation or other benefit. Nothing in the Program is investment advice, a promise of return or a representation about the future value of any asset.

### 3. Rules and parameters

The rules of the Program (rates, multipliers, caps, quests, seasons) are published in the application and may change. Changes apply from the following day and are not retroactive, except where a change corrects an error or addresses abuse. The record of points is kept in an append-only ledger; corrections are recorded as new entries with a reason.

### 4. Withdrawals

Withdrawing, redeeming or unstaking is never subject to any Program penalty. The only consequence is that time-based multipliers (streaks) restart.

### 5. Referrals

A participant may share a referral code. A wallet may be bound to one referrer, once, within thirty (30) days of its first sign-in, and only if it has no prior activity in the protocol older than thirty (30) days. A referral is considered converted when the referred wallet has minted QEURO and kept at least the minimum position for seven (7) consecutive days. Referral rewards are bounded as described in the application. Self-referral and referral rings are not permitted.

### 6. Leaderboards and public profile

Participants appear on leaderboards under a truncated wallet address. A nickname is shown only if the participant opts in. Participants may opt out of leaderboards and of the public profile at any time from their profile page.

### 7. Social tasks

Some tasks ask for a proof, such as a social network handle or a link to a public post. Proofs are reviewed manually by Quantillon Labs. Submitting false or borrowed proofs is not permitted.

### 8. Fair use and exclusion

Quantillon Labs may exclude a wallet from the Program, with the reason recorded, in case of self-referral, false proofs, coordinated manipulation, use of a sanctioned or blacklisted wallet, or any conduct that undermines the Program. Exclusion stops the accrual of points and removes the wallet from leaderboards and exports. Exclusion never affects any asset held on the blockchain.

### 9. Suspension and termination

Quantillon Labs may suspend or end the Program, in whole or in part, at any time. Recorded QP remain in the participant's ledger and can be exported from the profile page.

### 10. Personal data

The personal data processed for the Program, the purposes, the legal bases and the retention periods are described in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). Deleting your profile in the application removes your wallet from leaderboards and public profiles, anonymises referral links and deletes social proofs; the points ledger is kept under the public wallet address as described in the Privacy Policy.

These terms are governed by French law. Any dispute that cannot be settled amicably is submitted to the competent courts of Bordeaux, France, subject to mandatory consumer protection rules.
