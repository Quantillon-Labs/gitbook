Yield depends on strategy performance, protocol allocations, hedging and funding costs, fees and the timing of collection and release. An advertised or displayed annual percentage yield is an estimate or historical indicator, as described alongside it. It is not a guaranteed return, a daily payment to your wallet or a promise that yield will remain positive.

On redemption, you receive the amount then available for those shares under the applicable contract. Uncollected or unreleased yield is excluded, and redeemed shares do not participate in later releases.

Withdrawal availability depends on the selected vault's rules, liquidity, underlying strategies and safeguards. A yield release period does not by itself establish a lock on principal, but other technical or liquidity constraints may prevent immediate withdrawal.
